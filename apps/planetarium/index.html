    WELCOME TO THE SKY

    Stellarium Web Lab

    Your own sky observing tool, running in a web browser.

    The ‘All-sky map’ spreads the 360° sky over an elliptical Mollweide projection. Click a star or a constellation name to see its details. You can drag, zoom and search for objects, and ‘Full view’ resets the map. When you return to the sky dome, the previous field of view and the ground and atmosphere settings come back.

    Press ‘SPHEREx data analysis’ and a square showing the analysis field follows the cursor. Place the square where you want and click to look up SPHEREx data for that field. The default field is 3′ × 3′, and the view zooms in as far as needed for the field to be visible. The square is aligned to celestial north, so it may look tilted depending on the direction of the sky. Press Esc or the button again to cancel; opening an analysis returns you to normal selection.

    Choose the equatorial system under ‘Coordinate system’ or press the ‘RA/Dec’ button on the right to view an RA/Dec grid. Check the screen-centre coordinates, and enter a right ascension (hours) and declination (degrees) to move there. Grid, read-out, input and analysis coordinates are ICRS, corrected for aberration and the Sun’s light deflection.

    About this version

    A web project that uses the official Stellarium Web Engine. The sky position you click is converted to corrected ICRS coordinates and passed to the SPHEREx analysis screen. Looking up data needs an internet connection, and depending on the observed area and quality there may be no results. When you select a moving object, its apparent direction at the selected time is passed on as such; its past path is not tracked automatically.
