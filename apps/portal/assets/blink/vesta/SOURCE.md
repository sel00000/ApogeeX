# 4 Vesta blink (home page)

Copied unchanged from GitHub `sel00000/Apogee` (commit c6d676a), `apps/portal/moving/data/`:
`vesta_00.png`, `vesta_01.png`, `vesta_02.png`, `vesta.json`, `most_vesta.json`.

- Images: three real NASA SPHEREx DR1 Level-2 exposures (detector D6, 4.42–5.00 µm), 2025-08-04 11:05 → 2025-08-05 13:08 UTC,
  reprojected onto one north-up TAN grid (384 px × 6.15″ = 39.4′) with one common asinh stretch (display only).
  Original FITS URLs and SHA-256 are in `vesta.json`.
- Gold rings: NASA/IPAC IRSA MOST orbital predictions at each exposure time (`marker_x/marker_y`, PNG pixels,
  origin top-left, pixel centres at integers). Predictions, not measured positions.
- Identical (byte for byte) to `통합V2/moving/data/vesta*`.
